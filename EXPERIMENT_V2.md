# Experiment Design

The active experiment is V6. Its full scientific specification is
[STUDY_DESIGN_V6.md](STUDY_DESIGN_V6.md).

This filename is retained so older links do not break. The former V2 design
with nine cases, a technician role, and a separate `USE` action is no longer
active.

## One-sentence design

Participants inspect eleven scenes from controlled 10 x 10 maps and construct one
persistent shared rulebook; controlled counterexamples reveal whether broad
rules are refined, whether positive role enumeration is compressed with
negation, and whether a relational yielding convention is reused.

## Controlled variables

- identical `10 x 10` facility in every shift;
- identical four identities and roles, with off-duty robots parked;
- identical primitive menu;
- identical planning and animation rules;
- identical success criterion;
- only work assignment, starts, targets, and carried material change;
- shifts unlock in the theory-derived order;
- active workload progresses from one robot to two, then four.

## Main dependent measures

- first proposed rule;
- edits after each counterexample;
- first use of `IS NOT`;
- positive role enumeration versus `NOT cleaner`;
- unchanged reuse of the aisle convention at the machine;
- rule count, MDL, attempts, and time;
- regressions on previously solved shifts;
- obsolete or redundant rules retained at completion.

## Interpretation

The experiment studies candidate norm construction, not complete norm
emergence. The social content matters because every rule is universal, affects
several agents with different goals, can protect shared state or allocate
right-of-way, and must remain workable across situations.
